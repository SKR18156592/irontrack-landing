What it does

Four things your notes app can't do between sets.

Offline-first

Basement gym, dead zone, airplane mode. It still saves.

Every set is written to your phone the moment you tap it, so there's no loading spinner between you and the next rep. When you're back online, changes upload in the background and reach your other devices in seconds. Close the app mid-workout and your session is still there when you reopen it.

  • →Logging never waits on the network
  • →An unfinished session survives a reload or the phone closing the app
  • →Launches with no connection after the first visit

Smart double progression

Walk in knowing the number.

IronTrack reads your last session on the same equipment and suggests today's weight. Hit the top of the rep range on every working set and it adds about 2.5%. Inside the range, aim for one more rep. Most sets short of the range at failure, and it drops about 5%. One tap fills the weight in.

  • →Last time's numbers next to every set
  • →PR and ↑ Beat last time badges as you finish sets
  • →Estimated 1RM, RIR and set tags (warm-up, drop set, failure)
  • →Supersets, saved set presets and a rest timer that beeps

Nutrition & recovery

Targets that follow your body weight.

Set a goal (lose, maintain or build) and a weekly rate. IronTrack works out your calories and macros from your profile, gives training days a little more, and scales the meal plan to fit. Log your weight and it charts a 7-day average. If the trend drifts from your goal, it suggests a calorie change you can apply with one tap.

  • →Daily log for food, water and body weight
  • →Editable meal plans and your own foods
  • →Pre- and post-workout meal timing from your usual training time
  • →A muscle recovery map and weekly volume per muscle group

Privacy & cloud sync

Sign in only if you want sync.

Without an account, everything stays on your device. Sign in with email and password and your log follows you between phone, tablet and laptop. The database only lets each account read and write its own rows. Export your history as JSON or CSV, or take a full backup, whenever you like.

  • →Sessions logged on two devices while offline are both kept
  • →Signing out on a shared device clears that account's data from it
  • →No ads, no tracking scripts, no paywall

How it works

Built for lifters. Engineered for speed.

The phone is the source of truth. The cloud is a copy that keeps your devices in step.

LOCAL-FIRST

IndexedDB, not a loading spinner

Workout history and the nutrition log live in IndexedDB on your device, with no 5 MB cap. Reads come from memory, so screens render instantly.

NO FRAMEWORK

Vanilla JavaScript, three dependencies

Plain ES modules bundled by Vite. The only runtime libraries are the Supabase client, an icon set and confetti for your PRs.

SERVICE WORKER

The whole app is cached on install

Every file in the build is precached, so the app opens with no connection. Updates download only the files that changed.

SYNC THAT MERGES

Conflicts resolved, not lost

Sessions merge by id, deletions sync as tombstones, and the server sets timestamps, so a phone with the wrong clock can't hide a change.

FAQ

Questions lifters ask.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Does it really work offline?+

Yes. After your first visit the whole app is stored on your device. You can open it, log a full session, edit your plan and check your history with no connection. Changes upload when you're back online.

How does sync between devices work?+

Sign in with the same email on each device. Changes upload within a second of your last edit and other devices pick them up right away, or when they next come online. If two devices log sessions while out of touch, both sessions are kept. A deletion always syncs.

Do I need an account?+

No. Tap Continue without an account and everything stays on your device. You can sign in later from your profile to start syncing.

Who can see my data?+

Without an account, nobody: it never leaves your device. With an account, your data is stored in Supabase, and row-level security lets only your account read or write it. There are no ads or tracking scripts.

What does it cost? Is it open source?+

It's free, with no premium tier. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT license, and you can host your own copy on any static host with your own Supabase project.

Can I get my data out?+

Any time. Settings exports your workout history as JSON or CSV, and a full backup you can import on another device.

Does it work on iPhone?+

Yes. Add it to your home screen from Safari and it runs full screen, offline included. iPhones don't let web apps vibrate, so the rest timer beeps instead.
